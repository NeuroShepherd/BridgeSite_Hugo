1. Acceptance of These Terms

These Terms of Service (“Terms”) set the rules for using the BRIDGE and BRIDGE Gather (“GATHER”) applications, operated by the University of California, San Francisco (“UCSF,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). GATHER is the data-collection application that connects to the account your wearable device syncs to, retrieves your data through that platform’s API (for Fitbit, the Google Health API), and stores it on UCSF internal servers; BRIDGE is the application used by your care team to view that data, primarily during appointments. Your participation in the research study (the “Study”) is governed by your IRB-approved informed consent form, not by these Terms. These Terms do not change what you agreed to in your consent form and do not take away any of your rights. If you have questions about them, ask the study team before you connect your account.

2. Description of the Service

GATHER collects data from Study-assigned wearable devices (Fitbit, Oura, and Garmin) through those platforms’ APIs — for Fitbit, the Google Health API — runs the data pipeline, and stores the data on UCSF internal servers. BRIDGE makes that collected data available to the clinical and research team for research analysis, Study operations, and — where applicable — participant care, including during patient appointments. Both applications are provided for research and care purposes only.

3. Eligibility

Use of BRIDGE and GATHER is limited to individuals who have been enrolled in the Study through the IRB-approved process and have provided informed consent, and to authorized UCSF personnel. You may not use the applications if you are not an enrolled participant or an authorized user.

You agree to use the device assigned to you for the purposes of the Study and to follow any instructions provided by the Study team regarding wearing, charging, synchronizing, and returning the device. Devices remain the property of UCSF / the Study unless you are told otherwise, and may need to be returned at the end of your participation.

5. Device Connection and Authorization

To collect data, GATHER uses secure authorization (OAuth) provided by the wearable platforms. Google’s permission screen will show the app as “BRIDGE Gather” and ask for read-only access to your activity and fitness, health metrics, and sleep data, and optionally your time-zone setting. [Team to confirm: until Google completes its review of the app, Google first shows a “Google hasn’t verified this app” notice; the study team will show you how to continue.] By connecting your device, you authorize GATHER to access the specific categories of data you approve, for the duration of your participation, as described in the Privacy Policy. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your own wearable-platform account credentials.
